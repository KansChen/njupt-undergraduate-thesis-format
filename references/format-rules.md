# 南京邮电大学本科毕业设计（论文）撰写规范

Source: `本科生毕业设计（论文）格式要求.docx`, provided by the user. This reference captures the rules needed to create, format, or audit a NUPT undergraduate graduation design/thesis.

## Required Content

- The thesis should include: title, abstracts and keywords, table of contents, body, conclusion, acknowledgements, references, appendices when needed.
- Art-major requirements are handled separately and are not covered by this reference.

## Title

- Keep the title short and clear.
- The title should summarize the thesis content and professional characteristics.
- Prefer no more than 20 Chinese characters when possible.
- Use a subtitle when necessary details would make the main title too long.

## Abstracts And Keywords

- Chinese abstract comes before English abstract.
- The abstract should summarize the research viewpoint, main content, methods, results, and conclusions.
- Chinese abstract length should generally be about 300 Chinese characters.
- Keywords should be selected from substantive concepts in the title and content.
- Use about 5 keywords.

## Table Of Contents

- List the thesis components and body subheadings in order.
- Include page numbers after each heading.

## Body

- The body is the core of the thesis and may include theory, data, experimental methods, results, the author's arguments, conclusions, charts, photos, formulas, and related materials.
- Requirements: correct theory, clear logic, distinct hierarchy, fluent language, true and reliable data, correct formula derivation and calculations, concise and effective figures/tables.

## Conclusion

- The conclusion should be accurate, complete, and concise.
- Explain the position and role of the author's creative work in the research field.
- Objectively explain problems or limitations.
- Further work may be proposed.

## Acknowledgements

- Briefly thank organizations, supervisors, question-answering teachers, and other people who helped with research, design, and thesis writing.

## References

- In-text citations use superscript-style bracketed Arabic numerals, e.g. `[1]`, and must match the reference list.
- The reference list should include key books and literature, including author, title, publisher or journal, publication time, volume, issue, and page range where applicable.

## Appendices

Appendices supplement the thesis body and may include:

- Long formula derivations unsuitable for the body.
- Auxiliary mathematical work or repetitive charts helpful to readers.
- Computer program listings too long for the body.
- Materials valuable to specialists but unnecessary for general readers.

Appendix formatting:

- Place appendices after the body and continue page numbering from the body.
- Start each appendix on a new page.
- Label appendices with uppercase upright letters `A`, `B`, `C`, etc.
- Appendix labels such as `附录A` use bold SimHei, size 3.
- Number appendix figures, tables, formulas, and references separately from the body, prefixed by the appendix label, such as `图A1`, `表B2`, `式(C-3)`, `文献[D5]`.

## Discipline-Specific Requirements

- Theory research: include problem statement, premises or hypotheses, basic theory, mathematical model or theoretical proof, reasoning, calculation, application, verification, and analysis. Body length should be above 18,000 Chinese characters, except for work with unique insights.
- Engineering design: include design-plan justification, structural design, circuit design and parameter calculation, software development, equipment/component selection and use, and field testing. Body length should be above 15,000 Chinese characters, except for innovative designs.
- Software development appendices should include a software user manual of about 4,000 Chinese characters.

## Page Setup And Binding

- Use A4 paper.
- Margins: top 2 cm, bottom 2 cm, left 3 cm, right 3 cm.
- Header distance: 1.5 cm.
- Footer distance: 1.75 cm.
- Gutter: 0 cm, position left.

## Forms

- The cover is uniformly printed by the school and filled in with pen or signature pen.
- Task book, opening report, mid-term check form, supervisor evaluation, reviewer comments, grade sheet, excellent thesis application, and redo application should be filled in with size 5 SimSun and printed for signatures.

## Line Spacing

- Thesis content and all headings, including abstract, table of contents, acknowledgements, references, and appendices, use 1.25 line spacing.

## Fonts

- `摘要`, `目录`, `结束语`, `致谢`, `参考文献` use size 3 SimHei.
- `ABSTRACT` uses size 3 Times New Roman.
- Body level-1 headings use small size 3 SimHei.
- Body level-2 and level-3 headings use size 4 SimHei.
- Chinese body text uses small size 4 SimSun.
- English body text uses small size 4 Times New Roman.
- The source says detailed font requirements also depend on the graduation thesis template. Do not invent those template-only details if the template is not provided.

## Body Writing Format

- Chinese body text: small size 4 SimSun.
- English body text: small size 4 Times New Roman.
- Body header text: `南京邮电大学××××届本科生毕业设计（论文）`.
- Footer includes page number.
- Page numbers use Arabic numerals, bottom center, small size 5 Times New Roman.
- Write body by chapters.
- Level-1 chapter headings use `第一章`, `第二章`, `第三章`, etc.
- Each chapter starts on a new page.
- Chapter headings have no punctuation at the end and are centered.
- From level 2 onward, use Arabic numbering separated by periods, without punctuation after the final number, e.g. `1.1`, `2.1`, `3.1`; level 3 examples: `1.1.1`, `2.1.1`, `3.1.1`.
- Body headings normally do not exceed three levels.
- Every heading occupies a separate line.
- The source says detailed body format also depends on the graduation thesis template. Do not invent those template-only details if the template is not provided.

## Reference Formats

Journal:

```text
[序号] 主要责任者. 文献题名[J]. 刊名, 出版年, 卷号(期号): 起止页码.

```

Book:

```text
[序号] 主要责任者. 图书题名[M]. 其他责任者(如编者译者). 版本（第一版不用标出）. 出版地: 出版者, 出版年: 起止页码.
[2] 霍斯尼R K. 谷物科学与工艺学原理[M]. 李庆龙, 译. 北京: 中国食品出版社, 1989: 20-30.
```

Proceedings without extracted paper:

```text
[序号] 主要责任者. 文献题名[C]. 出版地: 出版者, 出版年: 起止页码.
```

Proceedings with extracted paper:

```text
[序号] 主要责任者. 析出文献题名[C]//文集编者姓名. 会议文集名. 出版地: 出版者, 出版年: 起止页码.
[3] 司宗国, 王群. 重子湮没快度关联的研究[C]//赵维勤, 高崇寿, 编. 第五届高能粒子产生和重离子碰撞理论研讨会文集. 北京: 中国高等科学技术中心出版, 1996: 105-107.
```

Standard:

```text
[序号] 标准编号, 标准名称[S]. 出版年.

```

Patent:

```text
[序号] 专利申请者. 专利题名: 专利国别, 专利号[P]. 公告或公开日期.
[5] 姜锡洲. 一种温热外敷药制备方案: 中国, 881056073[P]. 1989-07-26.
```

Electronic source:

```text
[序号] 主要责任者. 题名[EB/OL]. 发表或更新日期/引用日期(任选，前者用括号，后者用中括号). 电子文献的出处或可获得地址.
```

Newspaper:

```text

```

Dissertation:

```text
[序号] 主要责任者. 论文名[D]. 所属地: 所属单位, 出版日期.
```


## Foreign-Language Translation

- Complete a foreign-language translation of more than 20,000 printed characters.
- Chinese text uses small size 4 SimSun.
- Foreign text uses small size 4 Times New Roman.
- Print on A4 and bind separately.

## Binding Order

1. Cover, uniformly printed and issued by the Undergraduate School.
2. Task book.
3. Opening report.
4. Mid-term check form.
5. Main design/thesis section: originality declaration, abstracts and keywords, table of contents, body, conclusion, acknowledgements, references, appendices, etc.
6. Back cover, uniformly printed and issued by the Undergraduate School.

## Text Rules

- Chinese characters should use simplified Chinese characters from `简化汉字总表`.
- Use standardized Chinese characters.
- Text must be clear and must not be altered by hand after printing.

## Tables

- Tables include classification items and data.
- Classification items normally run horizontally from left to right; data runs vertically from top to bottom.
- Horizontal classification items must state symbols or units.
- Data columns should not use words such as `同上` or `同左`; write specific numbers or text.
- Table numbers use Arabic numerals by chapter, e.g. `表2.5`, `表10.3`.
- Each table should have a short, accurate title.
- Place the table number and title above the table.
- If there is no table title, place the table number at the upper-left or upper-right of the table, consistently throughout the thesis.
- Table numbering must be continuous with no duplicates or gaps.
- Table structure should be concise.

## Figures

- Select figures carefully.
- Figure numbers use Arabic numerals by chapter, e.g. `图1.3`, `图2.11`.
- Each figure should have a short, accurate title.
- Place the figure number and title below the figure.
- Coordinate symbols and abbreviations in figures must match the body text.
- Figure numbering should be consistent with table and formula numbering.
- Figure numbering must be continuous with no duplicates or gaps.
- Figures and text/symbols inside figures should be printed. If impossible, draw and label with pen or signature pen.
- For figures composed of subfigures, label subfigures with `a`, `b`, `c`, etc.
- Place figure number and title centered below the figure.

## Formulas

- Number formulas, equations, and expressions in the body.
- Formula numbers use Arabic numerals by chapter, e.g. `式(3-32)`, `式(6-21)`.
- Long formulas should be centered on separate lines and may only break at symbols such as `+`, `-`, `*`, `/`, `<`, `>`.
- Place the formula number at the far right of the formula line. If the formula wraps, place the number on the last line.
- Align continuous formulas at `=`.
- For integers over 999 or decimals with more than three places, separate groups with a half Arabic-numeral-space.
- For numbers less than 1, include `0` before the decimal point.

## Number Usage

- Use Arabic numerals for Gregorian centuries, decades, years, months, days, times, counts, and measurements.
- Do not abbreviate years. For example, write `1999年`, not `99年`.
- Write all significant digits for values.
- Preserve two decimal places when the value is expressed to two decimal places; for example, write `0.50:2.00`, not `0.5:2`.

## Software

- Software flowcharts and source program listings should follow software documentation format and be appended after the report/thesis.
- In special cases, they may be shown during defense instead of included in the report/thesis.

## Engineering Drawings

- Bind drawings of size 3# or smaller into the thesis report.
- Bind drawings larger than 3# separately as attached drawings according to national standards.

## Measurement Units

- Definitions and usage of measurement units follow the rules of the national metrology authority.
- Use internationally accepted symbols.
